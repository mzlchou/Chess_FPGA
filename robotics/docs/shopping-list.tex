{\LARGE\bfseries Chess robot shopping list}\par
\vspace{0.3em}
{\color{gray}November 27, 2026. Click a store name to open the part.}

Prices and stock weren't checked, so confirm the spec on each page before ordering. Links that
say \emph{search} go to a search because there are lots of equivalent sellers; pick one with
good reviews that matches the spec. \textcolor{green!45!black}{Green} rows are things we already
have or have already ordered.

\textbf{Budget:} the extrusion we found saves a big chunk of the frame cost, but bought new
this is still over the \$100 robotics budget. A used or broken Ender-3-style printer has the
motors, controller, belts, pulleys, lead screw, power supply and limit switches, so ask Nolop
and Bray for one before buying the gantry parts.
